## ⚙️ Included Scripts

- **`SnakeController.cs`**: The master AI script handling realistic slither locomotion, center/side whisker obstacle avoidance, target chasing, and the stinger strike coroutine.
- **`SnakeAntiphasing.cs`**: Prevents snake body parts/bones from overlapping and phasing through each other while winding.
- **`HeadLock.cs`**: Constrains and locks the head direction during target targeting and combat sequences.

## 🛠️ Setup Instructions

1. Import this folder into your Unity Assets directory.
2. Drag the `SnakeRgigedandTrypoFinal6 1` prefab into your scene.
3. Configure the `SnakeController.cs` script settings in the Unity Inspector (assign the targets, obstacle layers, and combat stinger rigs).
4. Run the game to see procedural slithering and combat dynamics.

Enjoy building your Empire!
